# About this folder

This folder holds the textbooks and papers that the candidate verbs were extracted from.
It is kept in the repository for transparency, so that anyone checking the work can see
exactly which sources the corpus was built from rather than taking a list on trust, and so
that the extraction step can be repeated on the same material.

Please note:

- These documents are the work of their authors and publishers and remain under their
  copyright and licence terms. They are collected here for research and verification
  purposes only.
- The MIT licence in this repository applies to our own code, data files and models. It
  does not apply to anything in this folder and grants no rights over it.
- If you want to reuse any of these publications, obtain them from the publisher and
  follow that publisher's terms.
- If you hold rights to a document here and would prefer it not be included, contact the
  corresponding author and it will be removed.

What the pipeline actually consumes is the extracted verb list, not the documents
themselves. The extraction is documented in the `BT 2` notebook, and the resulting
candidate pool with its full audit trail is in `revision_pipeline/`.
